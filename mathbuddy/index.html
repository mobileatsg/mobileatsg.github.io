We do not require registration, and we do not operate a social network, chat, or user-generated feed. We do not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, health data, or payment card numbers. Learning progress and settings are stored locally on the device (for example using on-device storage).

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions only as needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS) — optional permission to allow AdMob to use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for measurement / personalized ads. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the App still works and ads may still show in a non-personalized form.
  • Internet — required to load ads from Google’s servers.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or photo library access is required for core features.

8. Advertising

The App contains third-party advertisements from Google AdMob, including banner ads and interstitial ads. Ads help keep Math Buddy Kids free. Ad content is provided by Google and its partners; we do not control every creative. On iOS, we may request App Tracking Transparency. Declining tracking does not remove ads; it limits use of the advertising identifier for tracking as described by Apple and Google.

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform may present consent or privacy messaging related to ads.
