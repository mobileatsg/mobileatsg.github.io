3. Information we collect

Smart Alarm is a local-first alarm utility. Alarm schedules, labels, themes, pause dates, snooze preferences, pre-alarm notices, and any custom sound files you import are stored on your device (including an App Group shared container used so Live Activities can show countdown UI on the same device). We do not operate user accounts, and the App does not send your alarms or personal profile to Mobile@SG servers. If you use Report a Bug or Send Feedback in Settings, your device’s Mail app may open with diagnostic details you choose to send; that contact is voluntary and initiated by you.

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions only as needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

  • Alarms & Timers (AlarmKit on iOS) — required to schedule system alarms that can ring through Silent mode and Focus, and to show pre-alert countdown Live Activities.
  • Notifications — optional; used for pre-alarm / pre-wake notices where applicable. Manage in iOS/Android Settings.
  • Live Activities — used to show pre-alarm and snooze countdowns on Lock Screen and Dynamic Island (iOS).
  • App Groups (iOS) — used only to share limited alarm state between the main app and the Live Activity widget extension on the same device.
  • Alarm / notification related permissions (Android, when available) — may include notifications or exact-alarm related permissions as required for reliable ringing on that platform.
