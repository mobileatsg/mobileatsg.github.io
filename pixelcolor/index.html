We use information only for the purposes described in this policy, which may include:

  • Displaying banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads to free users (and banners for subscribers)
  • Processing optional yearly subscription unlock via Apple App Store or Google Play Billing
  • Responding to support requests you send us by email
  • Complying with legal obligations and protecting against abuse

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions only as needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

  • Internet / network — load ads and process subscriptions
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS) — optional permission so AdMob may use the advertising identifier for personalized ads; if you decline, the App still works and non-personalized ads may still appear
  • Advertising ID (Android) — used by AdMob where permitted by system settings
  • The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or precise location

8. Advertising

The App displays third-party advertisements through Google AdMob:

  • Banner ads — may appear for free users and yearly subscribers
  • Interstitial ads — may appear for free users on a timed basis; not shown to yearly subscribers
  • Rewarded ads — optional for free users who are out of daily Fill # or Hint uses (for example, watch an ad to earn extra Fill or Hints)

On iOS, the App may show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you select “Ask App Not to Track,” AdMob will not use your IDFA for tracking-based personalization; ads may still appear in a non-personalized form where allowed. On Android, you can limit ad personalization in system settings. Advertising partners may receive device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data as described in Google’s policies.

9. Purchases and payments

The App offers an optional auto-renewable yearly subscription (“Yearly Full Unlock”, product ID com.mobilesg.pixelcolor.unlock.yearly) that unlocks additional art collections and higher paint-tool limits, and removes interstitial ads. Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card number. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your platform account settings (Apple ID → Subscriptions, or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions).

10. Data retention

Progress and preferences are stored on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. We retain support emails only as long as needed to respond and for legitimate business or legal requirements. Advertising and payment data retained by Google or Apple is subject to their retention periods.

11. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing, except as disclosed in this policy (for example, when you interact with the advertising SDK).

AdMob and platform stores process data as independent controllers or processors under their terms when you use ads or make purchases.

12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed in connection with the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Where data is transmitted over the internet, we use encrypted connections (HTTPS) when communicating with our services.

13. Children’s privacy

Pixel Color is a family-friendly color-by-number game that may be enjoyed by children with a parent or guardian. We do not require accounts and do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own marketing databases. The App includes third-party advertising (AdMob). Parents and guardians can limit tracking and ad personalization in device privacy settings, supervise play, and contact us at mobileatsg@gmail.com if they believe a child has provided personal information that should be deleted.

14. International users
