3. Information we collect

Rain Pause is designed to work without collecting personal information from you. We do not require registration and do not operate an account system for this App. Preferences, session state, the Quiet Windows unlock flag, and review-prompt gates are stored on your device. The App may optionally fetch a public more-apps catalog over the network. Apple and Google process in-app purchases. We do not include an analytics SDK or advertising SDK. If you email us for support, we receive only what you choose to send.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID), or payment card details through the App. Core rain, sound, and settings work offline. If you email us for support, we will receive whatever information you include in your message.

5. How we use information

We use information only for the purposes described in this policy, which may include:

  • Providing the rain-window experience and remembering your on-device settings
  • Processing the optional Quiet Windows in-app purchase through Apple or Google so extra window scenes can be unlocked and restored
  • Fetching the optional public more-apps catalog
  • Responding to support requests you send us by email

6. Third-party services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Payments, store review prompts, and distribution are handled by Apple and Google. The optional more-apps catalog is a public JSON file hosted on GitHub Pages.

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions only as needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

  • Internet / network — optional more-apps catalog, in-app purchases, and opening store or developer links. Core rain works offline.
  • Vibration (Android) — optional haptics when you tap raindrops or wipe fog.
  • The App does not request App Tracking Transparency, advertising identifiers, camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location.

8. Advertising

The App does not display third-party advertisements.

9. Purchases and payments

The App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase called Quiet Windows that unlocks extra window scenes only. It is not an ads-removal product. Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card number.

10. Data retention

Preferences, session state, the unlock flag, and review-prompt gates stay on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Purchase records retained by Apple or Google follow their retention periods. Support emails we receive are kept only as needed to respond and for legitimate business or legal requirements.

11. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing, except as disclosed in this policy.

On-device preferences are not uploaded to Mobile@SG servers. When you tap a More apps listing, you leave the App and open the Apple App Store or Google Play product page for that title. Fetching the public catalog may involve standard network metadata processed by GitHub Pages.

12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed in connection with the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Where data is transmitted over the internet, we use encrypted connections (HTTPS) when communicating with our services.
