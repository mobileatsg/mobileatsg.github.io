4. Information we do not collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not operate a social profile for you in the App. We do not collect your name or email unless you send them to us. We do not collect payment card numbers. We do not embed Land Transport Authority (LTA) developer keys in the App. Location is optional: you can deny it and still use Board times.

5. How we use information

We use information only for the purposes described in this policy, which may include:

  • Providing live bus arrivals, remaining-ride weather and traffic, maps, and Live board updates
  • Remembering Board pins and Live settings on your device, and registering Live subscriptions on our servers when you turn Live on
  • Showing advertisements that help keep the App free
  • Responding to support emails you send us
  • Complying with law and protecting against abuse

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions only as needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

  • Location (when in use) — nearby bus stops and map centre. You may deny location; Board arrivals still work.
  • Notifications — optional Live board start alerts and lock-screen or notification updates. Live is off until you turn it on.
  • Network / Internet — load arrivals, weather, traffic, maps, Live updates, and ads.
  • Advertising ID (Android) — used by the ad SDK where permitted by platform settings.
  • Alarms and background operation (Android) — optional, so Live board can start around your leave time when the App is not open. You can refuse extra battery or exact-alarm settings; Live may then only run while the App can stay awake.

8. Advertising

The App displays third-party advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including banners on Board, Buses, and Map search lists, and occasional full-screen interstitials after you open a bus service detail a number of times. The map itself is kept free of banner ads. Interstitials are not shown when you return from Google Maps or Apple Maps.

The App requests non-personalized ads (npa=1). On iOS we do not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt; the Privacy Manifest declares that the App does not track users across other companies’ apps or websites. Ads may still use technical information as described in Google’s policies.

To report an inappropriate ad: open Settings → Report an Ad in the App, or use Google’s AdChoices / “Why this ad?” controls on the ad when available.

9. Purchases and payments

The App does not offer in-app purchases. If you purchased a paid app through Apple or Google, payment is processed by the platform; we do not receive your full payment card number. Board SG is free and supported by ads.

10. Data retention

Board pins, recents, and Live settings on the device remain until you remove them, clear app data, or uninstall. Live board device IDs, push tokens, and subscriptions on our servers are kept while Live is enabled so we can send updates in your chosen window; they are not used for advertising. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to respond and for legitimate business records. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies.

11. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing, except as disclosed in this policy (for example, when you interact with an advertising SDK, if any).

When you enable Live board, we store a device identifier, push tokens, and the stops and leave times you subscribed to on our servers so we can send Live updates. Those records are not sold. AdMob may receive device and advertising identifiers as described in Google’s policy. We do not share your Board pin list with advertisers.

12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed in connection with the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Where data is transmitted over the internet, we use encrypted connections (HTTPS) when communicating with our services.
