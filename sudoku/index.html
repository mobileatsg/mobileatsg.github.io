4. Information we do not collect

We do not require a name, email, or password to play. We do not operate a social graph, contacts access, camera, microphone, or location for core gameplay. We do not sell personal information. Free-play board progress is not synced to our servers.

5. How we use information

We use information only for the purposes described in this policy, which may include:

  • Provide Sudoku gameplay, settings, and local progress on your device
  • Show Google AdMob ads (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded interstitial continue on free play)
  • Optionally operate the Daily challenge leaderboard via Firebase Anonymous Auth and Firestore
  • Respond when you email support with a bug report or feedback
  • Improve reliability of ads and optional cloud features through SDK diagnostics

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions only as needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

  • Internet / network — load ads, optional leaderboard, and More apps catalog
  • Advertising ID (Android AD_ID; iOS App Tracking Transparency when prompted) — used by AdMob for ad measurement and personalization subject to your choices
  • No camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or photo library access for core play

8. Advertising

The App displays third-party advertisements through Google AdMob (banner and interstitial ads, and on free-play game over an optional rewarded interstitial to continue with +1 life). Daily challenge does not offer rewarded continue. Production releases use live ad units.

On iOS, if you are asked for App Tracking Transparency, you may allow or deny tracking. Denying tracking does not remove ads; AdMob may serve non-personalized ads. On Android, advertising identifiers are handled under Google Play and device settings.

9. Purchases and payments

The App does not offer in-app purchases. The App is free. Any past or future paid distribution would be processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your full payment card number.

10. Data retention

Local progress remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Leaderboard entries and anonymous Firebase identifiers are retained while needed to operate rankings and may be deleted or anonymized when no longer needed or upon a verified deletion request. Ad partners retain data according to their policies. Support emails are retained as long as reasonably needed to respond and for legitimate business records.

11. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing, except as disclosed in this policy (for example, when you interact with an advertising SDK, if any).

We do not sell personal information. Data is processed by Google (ads / optional Firebase) as independent controllers or processors under their terms when those SDKs run.
