The App is developed and published by Mobile@SG. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at mobileatsg@gmail.com.

3. Information we collect

You do not create an account. Puzzle progress (stars and settings) stays on your device. The App creates a random install identifier and sends it to our servers so helper balances and weekly Challenge results stay with that install. If you type a display name for the Challenge ranking, we store that name with your result. Google AdMob may process advertising and device data to show and measure ads. If you email us for support, we receive whatever you include in that message, including the diagnostics the App puts in the draft.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not require an account. We do not ask for your email address inside the App. We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or payment card numbers through the App. Share images are created on your device and are not uploaded by us.

5. How we use information

We use information only for the purposes described in this policy, which may include:

  • Delivering puzzle packs, helper balances, and the weekly Challenge
  • Showing a ranking when you submit a Challenge result
  • Showing and measuring advertisements that keep the App free
  • Responding to support messages you send us
  • Protecting the service from abuse of helper rewards

6. Third-party services

The App uses our own API and Google AdMob. We do not ship a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions only as needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

  • Network — to load levels, sync helpers, submit Challenge results, and load ads.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS) — optional. If you allow tracking, AdMob may use the advertising identifier to measure and personalize ads. If you ask the app not to track, the App still works and ads can still appear without that identifier.
  • Advertising ID (Android) — used by AdMob to show and measure ads. You can reset or limit it in Android settings.

8. Advertising

The App displays third-party advertisements through Google AdMob, including banners, interstitial ads after play, and optional rewarded ads for helpers. Ads are not a condition of playing the Beginner path. Personalized ads may use an advertising identifier where you allow it. You can limit ad personalization in iOS or Android privacy settings. We do not reward ratings, and we do not sell your information to advertisers.

9. Purchases and payments

The App does not offer in-app purchases. If you obtained a paid copy through Apple or Google, payment is processed by the platform; we do not receive your full payment card number.

10. Data retention

Puzzle progress stored only on your device remains until you reset it in the App or uninstall the App. We keep the install identifier, helper balance, and Challenge ranking data on our servers for as long as those features are offered and we need them to run the service. Advertising data is retained by Google under its own schedule. Support emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up.

11. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing, except as disclosed in this policy (for example, when you interact with an advertising SDK, if any).

Our servers receive the install identifier and, when you submit a Challenge result, the result and any display name you entered. Google receives ad-related data when an ad is requested or shown.

12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed in connection with the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Where data is transmitted over the internet, we use encrypted connections (HTTPS) when communicating with our services.

13. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience logic puzzle. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The easier “Beginner” boards are a learning path inside the same app, not a separate children’s service. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact mobileatsg@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps.

14. International users

We may process information in countries other than your own. Those countries may have different data protection laws. Where required, we take steps to protect information in accordance with this policy.

15. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of your personal information, or to object to certain processing.

  • Device permissions: manage in iOS/Android Settings
  • Advertising ID / personalized ads: manage in device privacy settings (where applicable)
  • Challenge display name: you choose whether to enter one; you can ask us to remove a submitted name
  • Support and privacy requests: email mobileatsg@gmail.com

We will respond to verifiable requests within a reasonable period, subject to applicable law.

16. Data deletion requests

Most puzzle progress is stored on your device. Uninstalling the App removes that local data. To request deletion of personal data we may hold (for example the install identifier, a Challenge display name, or information you emailed to support), contact mobileatsg@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request — Axiom”. Include the Device ID from a support email draft if you still have the App installed, so we can find the right install.
