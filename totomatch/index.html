1. Who we are

The App is developed and published by Mobile@SG. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at mobileatsg@gmail.com.

TOTO Match is a personal ticket checker for Singapore Pools TOTO Ordinary and Malaysia Sports Toto (Star 6/50, Power 6/55, and Supreme 6/58). You choose a market on first launch or in Settings. The App is not affiliated with Singapore Pools or Sports Toto and does not sell tickets, take bets, or pay prizes.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect your name, precise GPS location, contacts, or payment card details through the App. Ticket photos are used only for matching features you request. We do not collect Singapore Pools or Sports Toto account logins; you only photograph tickets you already have.

5. How we use information

We use information only for the purposes described in this policy, which may include:

  • Providing ticket scan, cloud match, history, and share features for Singapore TOTO and Malaysia Sports Toto
  • Applying the prize rules and official results for the market you selected
  • Managing free AI match credits, empty-upload limits (for example every few no-ticket photos may use a credit), and rewarded-ad grants
  • Sending optional push notifications when pending draw results are ready
  • Displaying third-party rewarded ads
  • Responding to support requests you send by email
  • Maintaining security and preventing abuse of the shared match service

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions only as needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

  • Camera / Photos — to photograph or select TOTO / Sports Toto ticket images for matching
  • Photo library (add) — optional save of match overview images
  • Notifications — optional alerts when match results are ready
  • Internet — to call the match service, load ads, and sync official draw data for Singapore and Malaysia
  • Advertising ID (where applicable) — used by Google ads for ad serving and measurement

8. Advertising

The App displays third-party rewarded interstitial advertisements from Google AdMob. You may choose to watch an ad to earn free AI match credits. Ads may use your device advertising identifier and related signals as allowed by platform settings and applicable law. You can limit ad tracking in iOS/Android privacy settings. Completing a rewarded ad may trigger a server-side verification callback to our servers solely to grant credits.

9. Purchases and payments

The App does not offer in-app purchases. If you purchased a paid app through Apple or Google, payment is processed by the platform; we do not receive your full payment card number.

10. Data retention

After AI extract, we delete the original upload photo from our servers. Structured match results (boards, prizes, status) and an optional numbered overview image may be kept for up to 90 days so we can rematch pending draws and, if you reinstall with the same install ID, offer to restore history without the original camera photo. Deleting a row in My matches removes that server record. Device install identifiers and credit balances are retained while you continue to use the service. Push tokens are kept until they change or become invalid. Support emails are retained as needed to handle your request. Uninstalling the App removes local device data. On Android, Google Backup (if enabled) may restore local history and photos after reinstall; that copy is stored under your Google account settings, not by us as a separate backup service.

11. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing, except as disclosed in this policy (for example, when you interact with an advertising SDK).

Ticket images and match results are sent to our match API so we can extract boards and score prizes for the Singapore or Malaysia market you selected. Advertising SDKs may receive device and ad interaction data as described by Google.
