4. Information we do not collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect your legal name, postal address, government ID, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or full payment card numbers through the App. We do not provide brokerage trading or order placement. If you email support, you may voluntarily include personal details in that message.

5. How we use information

We use information only for the purposes described in this policy, which may include:

  • Providing SGX market prices, watchlist, charts, announcements, dividends, and related App features
  • Storing favourites and preferences on your device
  • Creating and managing price alerts and delivering optional push notifications
  • Displaying advertisements to support a free App
  • Operating Remote Config feature flags (for example, optional menu entry points)
  • Diagnosing crashes and improving performance when analytics/crash tooling is enabled
  • Responding to support requests you send us by email

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions only as needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

  • Internet / network — load market data, announcements, and ads
  • Notifications — optional price alerts and announcement notifications (you can deny or disable in iOS/Android Settings)
  • Advertising identifier — used by the ads SDK where allowed by your platform privacy settings

8. Advertising

The App displays third-party advertisements using Google AdMob, including an adaptive banner and frequency-capped interstitial ads. Ad networks may collect or use device and advertising identifiers, IP address, and interaction data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted by law and your device settings (for example, App Tracking Transparency on iOS and advertising ID controls on Android). You can limit ad tracking in iOS/Android Settings. If no ad is available, the App continues to function without ads.

9. Purchases and payments

The App does not offer in-app purchases. If you obtained the App through Apple or Google, any platform fees or paid download (if applicable) are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your full payment card number.

10. Data retention

Favourites and display preferences are stored on your device until you change them or uninstall the App. Alert rules and device registration associated with your device identifier are retained on our backend until you delete the alerts, stop using the App, or request deletion where applicable. Push tokens are retained as needed to deliver notifications. Crash and diagnostic data retained by third-party providers is subject to their retention periods. Support emails are retained as needed to handle your request.

11. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing, except as disclosed in this policy (for example, when you interact with an advertising SDK, if any).

We share limited technical information with service providers who process data on our behalf (for example, cloud hosting, Firebase, and Google advertising services) solely to operate the App and show ads, subject to this policy and their terms.
