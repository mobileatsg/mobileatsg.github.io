We do not require an account or login. We do not sell your personal information. We do not knowingly collect payment card numbers. We do not operate a social network profile for you inside the App.

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions only as needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

  • Location (when in use) — center the map, sort by distance, and show nearby places. You may deny location; the App still works with a Singapore default center.
  • Network / Internet — load parking, EV, rates, maps, and ads.
  • Advertising ID (Android) — used by the ad SDK where permitted by platform settings.

8. Advertising

The App displays third-party advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including banners on some list screens and occasional full-screen interstitials. On iOS, the App requests non-personalized ads and does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt; the Privacy Manifest declares that the App does not track users across other companies’ apps or websites. Ads may still use technical information as described in Google’s policies. The main map view is kept free of banner ads.

To report an inappropriate or age-inappropriate ad, use Google’s AdChoices / “Why this ad?” controls on the ad creative when available, or contact us via Settings → Report a Bug / Send Feedback. Staging and debug builds use Google’s sample (test) ad units so developers do not click production ads.

9. Purchases and payments

The App does not offer in-app purchases. If you downloaded a paid product through Apple or Google in the future, payment would be processed by the platform; we would not receive your full payment card number. The current App is free and supported by ads.

10. Data retention

Saved places and preferences are stored on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to respond and for legitimate business records. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies.

11. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing, except as disclosed in this policy (for example, when you interact with an advertising SDK, if any).

Where data is transmitted over the internet, we use encrypted connections (HTTPS) when communicating with our services.
