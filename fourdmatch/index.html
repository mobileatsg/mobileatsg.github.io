1. Who we are

The App is developed and published by Mobile@SG. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at mobileatsg@gmail.com.

4D Match is a personal ticket checker for Singapore Pools 4D and Malaysia Magnum, Sports Toto 4D, and Da Ma Cai 1+3D. You choose a market on first launch or in Settings. The App is not affiliated with those operators and does not sell tickets, take bets, or pay prizes. Matching shows hit categories only (no prize money amounts).

4. Information we do not collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect your name, precise GPS location, contacts, or payment card details through the App. Ticket photos are used only for matching features you request. We do not collect Singapore Pools, Magnum, Sports Toto, or Da Ma Cai account logins; you only photograph tickets you already have. The App shows hit categories only and does not calculate or display prize money payouts.

5. How we use information

We use information only for the purposes described in this policy, which may include:

  • Providing ticket scan, cloud match, number bank, Results (live preview and official finals), and share features for Singapore and Malaysia 4D
  • Applying official draw results and bet-type expand rules for the market and operators you use
  • Managing free AI match credits and rewarded-ad grants (including server-side verification of completed ads)
  • Sending optional push notifications when match results are ready, live draws start, or bank numbers hit after finals
  • Displaying third-party banner and rewarded advertisements
  • Measuring how the App is used (anonymous Firebase Analytics events) and diagnosing crashes (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Responding to support requests you send by email
  • Maintaining security and preventing abuse of the shared match service

7. Permissions

The App may request device permissions only as needed for features you use. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; some features may not work without them.

  • Camera / Photos — to photograph or select 4D ticket images for matching
  • Photo library (add) — optional save of match overview images
  • Notifications — optional alerts for match ready, live draws, and bank hits
  • Internet — to call the match service, load ads, and sync draw data for Singapore and Malaysia
  • Advertising ID (where applicable) — used by Google ads for ad serving and measurement

8. Advertising

The App displays third-party banner and rewarded interstitial advertisements from Google AdMob. You may choose to watch a rewarded ad to earn free AI match credits. Ads may use your device advertising identifier and related signals as allowed by platform settings and applicable law. You can limit ad tracking in iOS/Android privacy settings. Completing a rewarded ad may trigger a server-side verification callback to our servers solely to grant credits. Core Results, number bank, and history features remain usable when ads fail to load.

9. Purchases and payments

The App does not offer in-app purchases. If you purchased a paid app through Apple or Google, payment is processed by the platform; we do not receive your full payment card number.

10. Data retention

After AI extract, we delete the original upload photo from our servers. Structured match results (status, hit categories, related metadata) and optional overview images may be kept so you can view My matches history and rematch pending tickets when official finals arrive. Deleting a row in My matches removes that server record. Number bank entries are retained until you delete them or stop using the service. Device install identifiers and credit balances are retained while you continue to use the service. Push tokens are kept until they change or become invalid. Official draw history we cache is public lottery data retained for matching (generally up to about one year per operator). Support emails are retained as needed to handle your request. Uninstalling the App removes local device data. On Android, Google Backup (if enabled) may restore some local data after reinstall under your Google account settings, not by us as a separate backup service.

We do not sell tickets, take bets, or pay prizes, and we are not affiliated with Singapore Pools, Magnum, Sports Toto, or Da Ma Cai.
